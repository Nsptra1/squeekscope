How to read this board
Aura comes from badges across three paths — Clout (sign-up, X/Discord links, invites), Code (Tester transactions and verified builder projects) and Capital (holder snapshots, +100 Aura per listed collection, max 500). The top of the board sits thousands of Aura above the community average shown on the left, and the top five alone hold — of all Aura in circulation — rank, not raw participation, is what the whitelist rewards.
